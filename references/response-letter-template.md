# Draft response letter template

Fill this in from the findings. Keep the reviewer's own comment numbering so their
list and this response line up item for item.

Construction language throughout: provide, coordinate, verify, connect, bond, route,
install, refer to, comply with, adjust as required. Avoid vague qualifiers unless the
uncertainty is deliberate and stated.

---

## Structure

```
TO:            [GC contact, company]
FROM:          [Reviewer, AHA Consulting Engineers]
DATE:          [date]
PROJECT:       [number and name]
SUBJECT:       [Submittal no. / RFI no.] - Response to [source] comments dated [date]
```

### Opening paragraph

Two or three sentences, no throat-clearing. State what was reviewed, what the original
disposition was, and how the response is organized.

> AHA has reviewed the [source] comments dated [date] against submittal [number],
> previously reviewed [date] with a status of [stamp]. Responses are organized by
> required action. Items 1 through N are addressed by existing content in the submitted
> package and require no further action by the manufacturer.

If a reopened-closed-item or schedule issue exists, name it here in one sentence. Once,
plainly, without heat.

### Section 1 - Addressed by the submitted package

For each: restate the comment briefly, cite the page and note, state the resolution.

> **Comment [N] - [subject].** The requested information is provided on page [x/y],
> Note [n], which states [quote or close paraphrase]. No revision required.

### Section 2 - Design documents to be revised

Own these plainly. A clean acknowledgment costs nothing and buys credibility for
everything in Section 4.

> **Comment [N] - [subject].** Comment is acknowledged. [What is being changed] will be
> revised on the [drawing/sheet] in the next issue, dated [date]. [Any information
> needed from the manufacturer to complete it.]

### Section 3 - Information requested from the manufacturer

Ask narrowly. Each item should be answerable with an existing document.

> **Comment [N] - [subject].** Provide [specific item]. Confirm [specific value or
> condition]. This information is required for [reason]; it does not affect release for
> manufacture.

### Section 4 - Comments requiring clarification of scope

This is the pushback section. The pattern that works: acknowledge the underlying need,
explain what the comment as written would require, offer the cheaper instrument.

> **Comment [N] - [subject].** The underlying need for [what they actually need] is
> understood. As written, the comment requires [what it would cost - revision of N
> sheets, factory engineering, a review cycle]. AHA recommends [the substitute
> deliverable], which provides the same information for [purpose] without a revision to
> the [drawing set / approved documents]. Confirm acceptance of this approach.

### Section 5 - Items requiring direction

Escalations. State the conflict, state the numbers, state what decision is needed and
from whom. Do not propose a resolution that is not ours to make.

> **Comment [N] - [subject].** [The requirement] conflicts with [the published limit].
> [The arithmetic, shown.] Resolution of [the requirement] is required from [owner /
> tenant / design team] prior to [milestone]. AHA is available to support that
> discussion.

### Closing

> Coordination of the above with the manufacturer's production schedule is referred to
> [GC]. Refer to the attached comment log for tracking.

## Phrasing that carries weight

**For declining a scope determination:**

> The design intent is [X], as shown on [sheet/detail]. Determination of contractual
> scope and cost is outside the scope of this response and is referred to the
> Construction Manager.

**For a limit that is not negotiable:**

> [Value] is the manufacturer's marked maximum. Equipment shall be installed in
> accordance with its listing; the overcurrent device shall not exceed this value.

**For separating requirement from preference:**

> [Item] is required by [code/specification section]. [Item] is recommended practice
> and is not a requirement of the contract documents. [Item] is a project preference
> and may be adjusted at the owner's direction.

**For a verification we are not making:**

> This is expected to be acceptable; verify [specific item] with [manufacturer / AHJ /
> responsible EOR] prior to [milestone].

**For a cross-discipline flag:**

> The published values indicate [the numbers]. This appears to conflict with [the
> requirement] and is referred to the [mechanical/plumbing] Engineer of Record for
> confirmation.

**For the schedule:**

> The manufacturer's selection carries a period of validity expiring [date]. Comments
> requiring a resubmittal cycle should be identified now so they can be consolidated
> into a single response prior to that date.
